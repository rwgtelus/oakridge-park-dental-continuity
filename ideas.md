# Oakridge Park Dental Continuity Site — Selected Design

## Fidelity Continuity

**Design movement:** Organic botanical healthcare, directly matching the established Oakridge Park Dental site.

**Core principles:** Preserve immediate brand recognition, reduce patient uncertainty, keep contact paths obvious, and remove all dependence on the unavailable Manus runtime.

**Colour philosophy:** Reuse the existing deep OPD forest green, dark green, muted sage, teal accent, cream, and white palette. No temporary campaign colours or generic maintenance-page styling.

**Layout paradigm:** A transparent header over a full-bleed photographic hero, followed by the original dark trust strip and alternating white/sage editorial sections. Interior pages use photographic page heroes and spacious card grids.

**Signature elements:** Original horizontal OPD logo, Playfair Display headings, DM Sans body copy, teal uppercase eyebrow labels, pill-shaped calls to action, rounded photography, numbered service rows, and a dark green footer.

**Interaction philosophy:** Every primary action resolves to a working call, email, page, or Google Maps link. The mobile navigation and sticky call action are simple, keyboard accessible, and dependency-free.

**Animation guidelines:** Restrained CSS transitions and scroll reveals under 300 ms, disabled when the visitor prefers reduced motion. No animation is required to understand or use the site.

**Typography system:** Playfair Display for headings; DM Sans for navigation, labels, body copy, contact details, and calls to action.
