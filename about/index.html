Our Practice

Our Family, Caring for Yours

Oakridge Park Dental was founded in 1985 and has grown alongside the Oakridge community. We remain focused on friendly, patient-centred dentistry in a comfortable setting where people feel heard and understood.

Located inside the South Tower of Oakridge Mall at West 41st Avenue, our practice welcomes patients of all ages from Oakridge, Cambie, Marpole, Kerrisdale, South Vancouver, and surrounding neighbourhoods.
