Your First Visit to Oakridge Park Dental

Choosing a new dentist is an important decision. Our practice has served the Oakridge community since 1985, and we welcome patients of all ages.

A new-patient visit may include a discussion of your dental and medical history, an oral examination, appropriate digital X-rays, an oral cancer screening, and a conversation about your questions and goals. The approved project guidance allows approximately 60–90 minutes for a comprehensive first visit.

What to Expect

Your New Patient Experience

01

Prepare

Bring valid photo ID, your BC CareCard or provincial health card, your dental benefits card if applicable, and a current medication list.

02

Comprehensive Exam

Your dentist will review your history, concerns, oral health, and any appropriate diagnostic images.

03

Personalized Plan

We will discuss findings, answer your questions, and outline recommended next steps based on your needs.

04

Plan Your Care

Our team will help coordinate follow-up visits and explain payment and receipt information.

Before You Arrive

Helpful Items to Bring

  • Valid photo identification
  • BC CareCard or other provincial health card
  • Dental benefits information, if applicable
  • A list of current medications
  • Recent dental X-rays, if available; a former office may email them to smile@oakridgeparkdental.ca

Payment Information

Non-Assignment Practice

Payment is collected at the time of your visit. The practice accepts Visa, Mastercard, debit, and cash; American Express is not accepted. A detailed receipt is provided for submission to your benefits provider.

Coverage varies by plan. Contact your benefits provider before your appointment if you have questions about reimbursement or eligibility.
